Install Aura

Flash the Aura weather display firmware to an ESP32-2432S028R (2.8" ILI9341 "CYD") from your browser. Works in Chrome or Edge only.

  1. Plug the board into your computer's USB port with a data cable.
  2. Click Install below and choose the "USB Serial" port.
  3. After it finishes, join the Aura Wi-Fi network and open http://192.168.4.1 to enter your Wi-Fi details.

Stuck at "Preparing to Install"?

  • Pick the port labeled "USB Serial".
  • Hold BOOT, tap RST, then release BOOT to enter flash mode.
  • On boards with both USB-C and Micro-USB, try each port, or try a different cable.
